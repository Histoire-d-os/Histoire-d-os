Each activity can be opened directly for a school or museum context.

Activity 1

Find the astragalus

Look for a foot bone in humans and in different animals.

Locate the talus/astragalus in the foot, understand its place in the tarsus and recognise that a comparable bone also exists in other mammals, even when their feet do not look like ours.

School Museum

Activity 2

Match a bone to an animal

Compare bones and animals to build a hypothesis.

Observe the shape of an astragalus, compare it with possible animals and justify a match using visible clues, while learning to say when identification remains uncertain.

School Museum

Activity 3

Achilles: a changing story

Compare images and stories to understand how a tradition changes.

Discover that the story of Achilles has not always been told in the same way, distinguish ancient narrative, later tradition and modern images, then understand why sources must be checked before interpreting an image.

Activity 4

One bone, several lives

Observe an object, suggest hypotheses and check what can really be known.

Learn to describe a bone or object before interpreting it, distinguish what is observed from what is supposed, and understand that one object can have several possible uses depending on context.
