Summary

Observe the shape of an astragalus, compare it with possible animals and justify a match using visible clues, while learning to say when identification remains uncertain.

What learners do

  • Observe before naming the answer.
  • Compare visible clues and justify a hypothesis.
  • Separate what is certain, probable or still to be checked.
