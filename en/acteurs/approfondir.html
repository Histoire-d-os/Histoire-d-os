How to read this page

Activity pages and public sources support further reading. Links to docs/ are working documents for transparency, not final classroom or mediation resources.

Scientific project

Histoires d'os studies the astragalus as an anatomical bone, archaeological object, possible game support, symbolic element, circulating word and starting point for inquiry.

Scientific validation to be finalized.

Misconceptions

The site avoids shortcuts: astragali are not turned into a universal origin story for dice, Achilles' heel is treated as later reception, and archaeological function is not inferred from shape alone.

Sources and credits

Public media must state whether they are historical sources, object photographs, diagrams, reconstructions, contemporary illustrations or generated images.
